OpenTTD 16.0-RC1 is now ready for testing with some minor bugfixes.

With a release candidate, the focus shifts from adding new features to finding and fixing bugs to make OpenTTD 16 the best yet.
The finding part is where you, the players, come in, too. If there's anything you notice, head over to our [issue tracker](https://github.com/OpenTTD/OpenTTD/issues/new/choose) and make a report.

Some minor changes over the previous beta5 release are:
* Updated OpenTTD TTF fonts to v0.9
* Fixed inconsistent rail station coverage area hightlight behaviour.
* Fixed uncommanded movement step after an end of line reverse could lead to path reservation problems or train crashes.
* Fixed uneven autoroad hotspot.

As always, see the changelog for the full list.
