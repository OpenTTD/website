We've had backwards trains, yes, but what about backwards ships?

We've got another round of bugfixes for you, and we couldn't help ourselves so have some features too.

<!-- more -->

The latest beta includes the following:

* NewGRF authors can now create double-ended ships that move backwards instead of turning around at each dock.
* You can now mesmerise yourself with Screensaver Mode, which follows random vehicles around your map. Access it under the (?) menu.
* Rocks now appear on coast tiles and in the sea, and spawn around lighthouses.
* Various other fixes and improvements, see the full changelog for details.

If you find any bugs, please [report them here](https://github.com/OpenTTD/OpenTTD/issues/new/choose).
